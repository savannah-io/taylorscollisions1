# 04 — Reviews Playbook

Reviews are the #2 ranking factor for the Local Pack (after distance + relevance). You're sitting at 140+ — that's competitive with United (759), well above Crash Champions (131) and Duluth Auto & Body (51). Once these are tied to your verified GBP, this becomes your moat.

## The math

| Lever | Why it matters | Easy to influence? |
|---|---|---|
| Review **count** | Volume signal | Yes — ask every customer |
| Review **recency** | Active business signal | Yes — keep them flowing |
| **Star average** (target 4.6+) | Quality signal | Yes — do good work |
| **Response rate** | Engagement signal | Yes — reply within 24 hrs |
| **Keyword presence in reviews** (e.g., "collision repair Duluth") | Topical relevance | Yes — see template below |

## Getting your review-request link

After your GBP is verified:
1. Sign into https://business.google.com
2. Click your Taylor's Collision listing
3. Click **"Get more reviews"** in the top menu
4. Copy the short URL — it looks like `https://g.page/r/Cxxxxxxxx/review`

Save this somewhere everyone on staff can grab it (sticky note at the counter, in your phone Notes, in a shop QR code).

## The "ask" — when and how

**When to ask:** the moment the customer picks up their car, while they're happy.

**Channel hierarchy (best → worst):**
1. **Text from your personal phone** — highest reply rate
2. **Email follow-up** — automated via the booking flow
3. **QR code on the receipt** — passive, captures the ones you forgot to ask
4. **In-person ask + write down the URL** — old school but works

### Text templates (use the customer's first name)

**The day they pick up:**
> Hey {first_name}! Max from Taylor's Collision. Mind taking 30 seconds to leave us a quick Google review? Means a lot. [link]

**Two days later if no review:**
> {first_name} — just a quick follow-up. If you have a minute, here's the review link: [link]. Thanks again!

**Don't:**
- ❌ Don't say "leave us a 5-star review" — Google's policy bans "review gating." Just ask for a review.
- ❌ Don't offer a discount in exchange — also against policy.
- ❌ Don't text people on Sunday morning — bad vibes.

### Email template

Subject: **Quick favor — Taylor's Collision**
Body:
```
Hi {first_name},

Thanks again for trusting us with your {vehicle}. Hope it's
running and looking great.

If you have 30 seconds, a Google review really helps a small
local shop like us — here's the link:

https://g.page/r/{your-id}/review

Max
Taylor's Collision
(770) 495-0050
```

## The reply — every single review, within 24 hours

Google explicitly weighs **response rate** in ranking. Plus, future customers read your replies more than the reviews themselves — your reply is a 50-customer audience, not just one.

### For 5-star reviews (template)

> Thanks so much, {name}! Glad we got the {vehicle / specific detail} sorted out. Tell your friends in {city} we'll take great care of them too. — Max

**Personalize** every reply with:
- Their name
- A specific detail from the review or visit
- Mention of the city they're from (good SEO signal)

### For 4-star reviews (template — turn into 5)

> {name} — thanks for the honest feedback. Glad we got most of it right. If there's anything that kept it from being a five-star experience, shoot me a text at (770) 495-0050 and let's make it right. — Max

### For 3-star and below (template — defuse + offer to fix)

> {name} — sorry to hear this. That's not the standard we want. I'd like to make it right — please call me directly at (770) 495-0050 or email info@taylorscollision.com so we can figure this out. — Max

**Never:**
- ❌ Argue in the reply
- ❌ Reveal customer info (cars, addresses)
- ❌ Get defensive

If a review is fake/abusive/from a non-customer:
- Flag in GBP → Three dots → Report review → "Off-topic" or "Conflict of interest"
- Most fakes get removed in 5–10 days
- If Google won't remove it, your honest reply is the best signal to future customers

## Cadence

| Frequency | Goal |
|---|---|
| Every customer | Ask for a review at pickup |
| Daily | Reply to any new reviews |
| Weekly (Friday) | Batch-text 5 customers who haven't reviewed yet |
| Monthly | Check star average + review volume vs competitors |

## Tracking sheet

Make a simple Google Sheet:

| Date | Customer | Vehicle | Reviewed? | Reply sent? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Update it weekly. After 90 days you'll see which customers consistently review (those are your VIPs — treat them well).
