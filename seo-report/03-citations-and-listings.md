# 03 — Citations & Directory Listings

A **citation** is any place online that mentions your **N**ame, **A**ddress, **P**hone (NAP) — even without a link. Google uses NAP consistency across the web as a trust signal. Inconsistent NAP = lower local ranking.

## The canonical NAP block (copy this exactly, everywhere)

```
Taylor's Collision
2785 Buford Hwy Ste 101-C
Duluth, GA 30096
(770) 495-0050
https://www.taylorscollision.com
```

Hours:
```
Monday    8:30 AM – 5:30 PM
Tuesday   8:30 AM – 5:30 PM
Wednesday 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM
Thursday  8:30 AM – 5:30 PM
Friday    8:30 AM – 5:30 PM
Saturday  Closed
Sunday    Closed
```

> **Important:** Always use **"Ste 101-C"** (with hyphen), not "Suite 101C" or "Unit 101" — Google treats these as different addresses.

## How to do this efficiently

**Manual approach (free, takes ~2–3 hours):**
1. Open a spreadsheet with columns: `Site | URL | Status | Date Submitted | Login Email | Notes`.
2. Work through Tier 1 in one sitting (~45 min).
3. Tier 2 in another (~30 min).
4. Tier 3 and 4 spread across a few days.

**Tool-assisted approach ($):**
- **BrightLocal** (~$29/mo) — citation tracker + auto-submit to ~50 directories. Worth it if your time is more valuable than the cost.
- **Moz Local** ($14/mo) — similar.
- **Whitespark** — one-time citation cleanup service ($300–600).

## Audit & cleanup (do once a quarter)

Use these free tools to catch NAP mismatches across the web:

- **Moz Local Search Check:** https://moz.com/local/search — enter your NAP, get a list of where you're listed and where there are mismatches.
- **Yext PowerListings Scan:** https://www.yext.com/pl/free-listings-scan — similar.
- **Whitespark Local Citation Finder** — for tracking competitors' citations (free tier).

When you find a mismatch, log in (or email support) and update to the canonical NAP.

## What to NOT do

- ❌ Don't submit to spammy "1000 directories for $5" services — links from sketchy sites can hurt.
- ❌ Don't put a slightly different phone number on different sites (a tracking number, etc.) — Google reads it as a different business.
- ❌ Don't list two locations on the same listing — split into two GBP listings if you ever expand.
