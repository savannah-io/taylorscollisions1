## What you should NOT do (common local-SEO traps)
- ❌ Stuff keywords into the GBP business name ("Taylor's Collision — Duluth Auto Body Repair") — Google penalizes and competitors will report you.
- ❌ Pay for "guaranteed Google rankings" — every legit local SEO firm tells you Google doesn't sell rankings.
- ❌ Buy fake reviews — Google detects these and suspends listings.
- ❌ Create duplicate GBP listings ("Taylor's Collision Duluth" + "Taylor's Collision Auto Body") — Google merges or suspends both.
- ❌ Change your address frequently — kills trust signal.
- ❌ Use a virtual office / PO box as the GBP address — Google requires a real, staffed location.
