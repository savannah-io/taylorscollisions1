## TL;DR

Your website is **not** the bottleneck — the on-page SEO is solid (LocalBusiness JSON-LD, geo tags, areaServed, FAQ schema, NAP, sitemap all in place). The reason you don't show up in the map pack today is **off-site**: no verified Google Business Profile, no major-directory citations, and reviews aren't on your GBP. Fix those and you'll show up.

Priority — in this order:

| # | Action | Owner | Time | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Claim & verify Google Business Profile** | You | 10 min setup + 5–14 day postcard wait | 🔥 Critical — unlocks the map pack |
| 2 | **Get ~30 fresh Google reviews on the GBP** | You + customers | 2–4 weeks | 🔥 Critical — drives ranking inside the pack |
| 3 | **Build NAP-consistent citations** (Yelp, BBB, Yellow Pages, AutoMD, etc.) | You | 2–3 hours | High — confirms business legitimacy |
| 4 | **Reply to every Google review within 24 hrs** | You | 5 min/day | High — Google explicitly weighs response rate |
| 5 | **City landing pages** (DONE — shipped) | Me | done | Medium — captures `collision repair {city}` long-tail |
| 6 | **Local backlinks** (Chamber of Commerce, news, partner shops) | You | Ongoing | Medium — slow but durable |
